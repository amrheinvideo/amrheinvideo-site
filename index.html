FAQ

Good questions, answered

The things creators usually want to know before reaching out.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the length and complexity of your videos and how often you upload. After a quick conversation about your channel, I put together a clear scope with fixed pricing so you know exactly what you are paying before any work starts.

What is your turnaround time?

Most edits land within a few days, depending on length and how much is on my plate that week. We agree on the timeline in the scope step, and I deliver on the dates we set. No vanishing acts.

How many revisions do I get?

Unlimited. We keep refining until you are happy with every frame. Your name is on the video, so it has to be right.

What do you need from me to start?

Your raw footage, any channel assets you want used (logos, intros, fonts), and a reference video or two so I can match your style. If you are not sure what your style is yet, we figure that out together.

What types of videos do you edit?

Gaming first: let's plays, reviews, discussions, and live stream cuts, plus hardware and tech content. If your channel lives in that world, we are a good fit.

Do you take on ongoing work?

Yes. One-off projects are welcome, but most of my work is ongoing partnerships with creators who upload regularly. If you want a reliable editor in your corner long term, that is exactly what I am here for.
